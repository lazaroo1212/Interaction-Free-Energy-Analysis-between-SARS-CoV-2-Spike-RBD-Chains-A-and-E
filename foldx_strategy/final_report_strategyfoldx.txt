1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------
This study performed a computational energetic analysis of the interaction between the SARS-CoV-2 Spike Receptor Binding Domain (RBD, Chain E) and the human ACE2 receptor (Chain A). Using the FoldX algorithm, we repaired the crystal structure (PDB: 6M0J), determined the baseline binding affinity, mapped critical interface residues (hotspots), and evaluated the impact of three epidemiological variants (N501Y, E484K, K417N).

2. STRUCTURAL PREPARATION (Step 1)
----------------------------------
* Objective: To remove steric clashes and minimize Van der Waals repulsions in the crystal structure.
* Input: 6m0j.pdb (Raw PDB)
* Command: RepairPDB
* Outcome: The structure was successfully minimized (6m0j_Repair.pdb). This step ensures that subsequent energy calculations reflect biological interactions rather than crystallographic artifacts.

3. BASELINE INTERACTION ANALYSIS (Step 2a)
------------------------------------------
* Objective: To quantify the stability of the Wild Type (WT) complex.
* Command: AnalyseComplex
* Result (Delta G Binding): -17.18 kcal/mol
* Interpretation: The WT complex exhibits high stability. This value serves as the reference point (0.0 kcal/mol) for all mutational comparisons.

4. INTERFACE MAPPING & HOTSPOT IDENTIFICATION (Step 2b)
-------------------------------------------------------
* Objective: To identify key residues contributing most to the binding energy via Computational Alanine Scanning.
* Methodology: Mutating interface residues to Alanine and calculating DDG (Delta Delta G).
* Criteria: DDG > 1.5 kcal/mol indicates a "Hotspot".

* Top 5 Critical Residues Identified:
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  | Rank | Model ID  | Residue Identity           | DDG (kcal/mol)    |
  |------|-----------|----------------------------|-------------------|
  |  1   | Repair_18 | Tyr 41 (Chain A - ACE2)    | +5.60 (Critical)  |
  |  2   | Repair_13 | Tyr 83 (Chain A - ACE2)    | +4.66 (Critical)  |
  |  3   | Repair_37 | Arg 357 (Chain A - ACE2)   | +4.43 (Critical)  |
  |  4   | Repair_20 | Arg 403 (Chain E - Spike)  | +4.35 (Critical)  |
  |  5   | Repair_10 | Tyr 505 (Chain E - Spike)  | +3.95 (Critical)  |
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

* Biological Insight: The interface is dominated by aromatic (Tyrosine) and electrostatic (Arginine) interactions. Tyr 505 on the Spike protein is identified as a crucial anchor for cell entry.

5. VARIANT ANALYSIS (Step 3)
----------------------------
* Objective: To predict the impact of specific mutations found in Alpha, Beta, and Gamma variants.
* Command: BuildModel + AnalyseComplex

* Results Table:
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------
  | Variant | Mutation | Binding Energy (DG) | DDG vs WT | Effect        |
  |---------|----------|---------------------|-----------|---------------|
  | WT      | -        | -17.18 kcal/mol     |  0.00     | Reference     |
  | Var 1   | N501Y    | -16.99 kcal/mol     | +0.19     | Neutral       |
  | Var 2   | E484K    | -17.52 kcal/mol     | -0.34     | Stabilizing   |
  | Var 3   | K417N    | -15.11 kcal/mol     | +2.07     | Destabilizing |
  --------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Interpretation:
  - K417N: Causes a significant loss of affinity (+2.07 kcal/mol) by disrupting a salt bridge with ACE2-D30. This suggests K417N is selected primarily for immune escape, despite the cost to ACE2 binding.
  - E484K: Slightly improves affinity (-0.34 kcal/mol) through electrostatic optimization.
  - N501Y: Predicted as neutral in rigid-body docking, though experimental data suggests stabilization via pi-stacking (a known limitation of static FoldX rotamers).

6. CONCLUSION
-------------
The FoldX pipeline successfully identified the energetic determinants of the Spike-ACE2 interface. The analysis confirms that the interaction relies heavily on a few critical hotspots (Tyrosines and Arginines). Among the tested variants, K417N was identified as structurally deleterious to binding, while E484K was predicted to enhance the interaction.
